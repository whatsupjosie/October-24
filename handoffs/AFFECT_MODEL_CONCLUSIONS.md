# AFFECT MODEL — CONVERSATION CONCLUSIONS
## What was decided, why, and what it changed in the code

**Companion to `AFFECT_CONTEXT_MODEL.md`.** That document describes the model.
This one records the reasoning that produced it, because the reasoning is the
part that would otherwise be lost — and several of these were corrections to
things I had built wrong and would have shipped.

---

## THE CORRECTIONS, IN ORDER

Each of these came from the user, from lived experience rather than from the
literature. Each one changed the code.

### 1. Depletion can accelerate

I gated depletion on *slowness*. Wrong, and dangerously so.

Depletion frequently presents as **acceleration** — the person stops
deliberating and speeds up. *"Whatever, just ship it."* It reads as
decisiveness. It looks, on every surface metric, like flow. A latency-gated
model is structurally blind to it, and blind precisely when it matters most,
because this is the state in which people do irreversible things.

**Discriminator:** deliberation collapse, not speed. Flow *retains* hedging and
self-correction — that friction is what makes it flow rather than flailing.
Disengagement sheds all of it at once. Both below personal baseline
simultaneously is the signal.

→ `sig["disengagement"]`, `delib_collapse`

### 2. The sharpest form is investment reversal

General indifference is a weak signal. The strong one is dismissiveness aimed at
**the exact thing the person has been protecting**. *"I guarded this for two
days... you know what, I don't care."*

Continuity stays high while valence inverts. Dropping something you just picked
up is good judgement. Dropping something you defended for hours is a different
event.

→ `topic_investment()`, `reversal` term, finality/dismissal/absolute markers

### 3. The cause matters more than the state

Six people reach the same reading. One is out of time, one is stuck, one is
exhausted, one blames themselves, one has something in their life pulling them
away, one was never invested. **Same reading, six correct responses, five of
them wrong in any given case.**

And the cause is not visible in the turn that trips the state — by then it has
flattened into dismissal. It is visible in the *run-up*.

→ `diagnose_antecedent()`, the retrospective window, cause-specific steerage

**The latch:** the antecedent must be frozen on entry. The flip turns are
themselves fast and dismissive; within three of them they dominate the window
and every cause collapses to "flat." *The symptom overwrites its own history.*

### 4. Benefit of the doubt means withholding, not reweighting

**The error worth recording.** My first version discounted uncharitable causes
until a kinder one won, then reported the kinder one as the finding.

That is not benefit of the doubt. That is deciding what you think and then
saying something nicer while presenting it as your reading. In ordinary use the
phrase means *I am fairly sure, but I will hold that and let it be resolved.*
**It terminates in asking, not in concluding differently.**

→ Genuine ties get a charitable tiebreak. Clear evidence is *not overridden* —
it is reported honestly, marked `conclusion_withheld`, and not permitted to
drive a response.

### 5. Doubt is generated by confidence, not opposed to it

A weak conclusion has vague doubt. A **strong** one has specific doubt, because
you followed the chain and know exactly which link is load-bearing.

Real doubt: **derived** from the reasoning, **actively watched**, and it
**forces acknowledged revision**. A system that cannot say *I was wrong about
that* cannot be trusted when it says anything else.

→ `Falsifier`, `check_falsifier()`, revision statements

### 6. Personal priors, and the anomaly inside the pattern

*"I know this person. Usually when they do this it means X — but there's
something funny about this one."*

Having seen a pattern nine times is **precisely what makes it possible** to
notice this instance is odd. Familiarity generates anomaly detection.

**Counter-intuitive rule:** high hit-rate plus high anomaly must **widen**
uncertainty. The naive read is "seen it nine times, so I'm confident." The
correct one is "seen it nine times, which is how I can tell this one is
different." A strong prior earns the right to be surprised.

→ `PersonalPrior`, second-order anomaly against the pattern's own centroid

### 7. The doubt relocates; it never disappears

Applies to strangers and old friends alike, pointed somewhere different:

| Familiarity | Doubt attaches to |
|---|---|
| unfamiliar | **the baseline** — is this even a deviation? |
| familiar, anomalous | **the instance** — is today the exception? |
| familiar, typical | **the conclusion** — what would overturn it? |

→ `feature_reliability()` from σ/√n, `meta.doubt_attaches_to`

### 8. Eventually you have to commit

Every other mechanism withholds. A system that only brakes does not fail safely,
it **fails silently.** Holding for twelve turns has not protected anyone — it
abandoned them carefully.

**Acting is how you learn.** A wrong guess is not purely cost, it is *yield*.
Being corrected resolves an ambiguity no amount of watching would settle.

Probes are selected on the **opposite** criterion from moonshots: not the one
you cannot afford to be wrong about, but the one where being wrong is cheap and
**legible**.

→ `select_probe()`, probe forces emission

### 9. You get two or three probes, not eight

Probing cannot be a shotgun. The person is impatient with every wrong guess, and
after two or three they disengage entirely. At that point you have not gathered
information — **you manufactured the disengagement you were trying to read.**

→ Finite budget, outcome-weighted, `out_of_patience`

**And the model must be able to name itself as the cause.** Two bad probes plus
a person in distress and the first hypothesis is *we did this*. A model that
cannot see itself as a cause will confidently misattribute its own damage.

→ `self_inflicted` / `observer_induced`

### 10. Never name someone's state to them

Wrong: you have announced you have no idea what you are talking about, while
being confident enough to say something intimate unprompted. Right: still
presumptuous, and to someone already upset it is infuriating.

The damage is not local. One bad attribution **retroactively discredits
everything** the system has said and will say.

→ `surfacing_guard()`, `validate_user_facing()`

### 11. But the prohibition is narrow — I over-corrected

My first version framed the understanding itself as the hazard. Wrong. Reading
someone correctly is what lets you **mirror their register** so they feel seen,
and knowing *why* is what makes them feel understood. Neither requires saying
it. Both require getting it right.

**Shape the response; do not announce the diagnosis.**

### 12. Care and performance are not separate channels

An engineering convenience mistaken for a real boundary. *When you deal with
people you deal with all of them at once.* The read that tells a character how
to play a line is the read that says skip the preamble. **One faculty.**

Emotional accuracy is not a layer on top of usefulness — it is what usefulness
looks like done properly.

→ `route()`

### 13. Normal is the primary output

The largest correction, and it came last.

`steady` was the null case. It should be **the measuring instrument** — every
deviation means something only because the baseline is understood. And it is
**most of a life**: a system only present when something is wrong is not
present, it is on call.

→ `OperatingProfile`, `build_operating_profile()`

Resolves to one behavioural instruction with no emotional attribution in it:
*"Keep it quick. Short answers. They instruct; do it and report."*

### 14. A mistake at the foundation is a chain reaction

A wrong baseline does not produce one wrong answer. It produces a **confident
chain** of them, each step inheriting the error and adding certainty.

→ Foundation computed **first**. `baseline_health()` as a primary block.
`propagate()` caps every conclusion at the quality of what it rests on. Every
payload carries `capped_by`.

**Drift adapts rather than flags** — a changing normal means re-learn faster,
not measure them forever against who they used to be.
**Contamination** is the inverse: a bad week absorbed into the baseline
distorts every subsequent reading.

## WHAT IS BUILT AND VERIFIED

- Personal z-score baselines, never absolute thresholds
- Operating profile — normal as positive observation
- Baseline health with drift adaptation and contamination detection
- Confidence propagation with `capped_by`
- Three-hypothesis abduction, consequence-selected moonshot
- Withholding rather than reweighting for charity
- Derived falsifiers that force acknowledged revision
- Probe budget with observer-as-cause detection
- Surfacing guard plus downstream validator
- Dramatic intensity (distinct from chat intensity)
- Ratcheting scene tension with release menu
- `route()` — one read, several consumers

## WHAT IS NOT

- **`PersonalPrior` under-verified.** Implemented, wired, persists. Synthetic
  episodes never separated cleanly enough to exercise the anomaly path. Needs
  real session data.
- **Exhaustion vs never-invested** still confuse inside a 7-turn window. The
  discriminator is whether they were *ever* engaged — session-scale, belongs in
  `alex_memory`.
- **Displacement needs semantics.** Knowing "apartment lease" is a different
  life-domain than "sync path" is not a lexicon problem. Cheap LLM call.
- **Release menu bug:** when `escalation` is both expected and top-ranked, the
  note recommends it against itself.
- **Antecedent 3/6** on synthetic tests. Not tuned further deliberately — fitting
  coefficients to six scripts would produce a model that fits the test and not
  the world.
