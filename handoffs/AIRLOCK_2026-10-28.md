# The airlock: waiver, avatar check, detention, sealed records, 2026-10-28, Claude

## Reason

Josie, 2026-10-28, designed how guests enter someone else's PubCast and asked for it to be written to code:

- Joining someone else's PubCast puts you in the waiting room. It has no links and no hotspots. You get in only when someone inside pulls you in. The owner of a copy starts inside, usually in the studio or the dressing room.
- In the waiting room you sign the waiver. It covers being recorded anywhere but the dressing room, your avatar's thoughts being recorded so an incident can be traced from its start, and avatars communicating in English or a language translatable to English. The owner signs, and the AI signs as well.
- Then the avatar has to meet the host's requirements. A guest can try avatars on in the foundry instead.
- The waiting room is also the timeout. The guest side is kept apart from the detention side. Detention is recorded completely, everything thought or said.
- A timeout is followed by a talk with the person and an agreement not to do it again. Technical problems go to the waiting room too.
- The guest side has a foundry room where guests try avatars, and their Cricket helps the AIs learn the controls.

The other pieces agreed in that conversation are in too:

- the foundry is a no-record room
- penalties follow the owner, not the avatar
- detention has no foundry access
- avatars fail with reasons and a fix, not a flat no
- an avatar that already passed is remembered
- the ladder (warning, timeout, boot) is written into the waiver
- there's a visible timer, a staff pass, a waiting timeout, leaving at any time, and the host leaving sends guests back to the waiting room
- thought records are sealed and opened only through an incident

## The waiver text is a draft

The wording in `DEFAULT_WAIVER` (`modules/airlock.py`) is Claude's draft so the mechanism could be built and tested. Josie should rewrite it. ## Decisions made, for Josie to confirm or change

- **Thoughts follow the camera rule.** Nothing is recorded in the dressing room or the guest foundry, not even thoughts. The exception is detention, where everything is always recorded. An incident that starts in the dressing room will therefore have no record. Change `NO_RECORD_ROOMS` if thoughts should be kept there too.
- **Recording starts at signing.** Before a person signs, only the signature event itself is recorded.
- **A timeout's clock keeps running if the person leaves.** Leaving doesn't clear the timeout. Coming back puts them straight back in detention, and release still needs the host and a fresh signature from the owner and the AI.
- **A damaged log is never written past.** If the last record of a sealed log doesn't match its hash, nothing more is appended, so new records can't paper over the break. The damage is flagged in the audit log. If the break is in the middle, recording carries on, and every later check still reports the break.
- **The language rule has two parts.** The AI declares a language, which must be on the host's list; English can't be removed from it. Speech is also screened for hidden characters, encoded runs and text that is mostly symbols. The screen cannot translate anything or prove a message is translatable. A flagged message is still recorded, and the flag goes into the audit log.
- **Wearable avatars:** your own, and the host's house avatars. Nobody else's.

## Found, not fixed: comma bug in the shared blackbox payload encoder

`blackbox_event_language/schema.py` `_encode_payload` leaves commas unescaped, but `_decode_payload` splits on commas. Any payload value containing a comma, such as ordinary speech, writes and verifies correctly but decodes into fragments. The airlock works around this by fully percent-encoding its own values (`"enc": "q1"`), and `test_commas_and_newlines_in_speech_survive_the_sealed_record` proves the round trip.

Other writers, including `modules/blackbox_runtime.py` chat transcripts, still hit the bug. The shared schema was left unchanged because fixing it changes the wire bytes of new records, and that needs its own pass with the blackbox tests.
