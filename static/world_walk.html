Reference cans

A 12 oz Coke can is 4.83 in tall and has not changed in 70 years. Add one, drop it on any surface (floor, table, lamp, bin), and drag its top handle until it looks like a real can there. Put one near the front, the middle and the back if you can. One can sizes the whole picture the same everywhere; three give it a slope. If the can is on a table or a lamp, press "Set floor point" and click where that thing meets the floor.
